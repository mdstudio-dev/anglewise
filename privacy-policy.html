Privacy Policy — Anglewise

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Anglewise ("the app") is made by Ouayat Studio (contact: ouayatstudio@gmail.com). This policy explains what happens with information when you use the app.

What the app reads on your phone

To work as a bubble level, inclinometer and protractor, the app reads your phone's motion sensors (accelerometer and gravity sensor). These readings:

  • are read only while the app is open,
  • are shown on your screen and used to calculate angles,
  • are not saved as a history, not sent to us and not shared with anyone by the app.

The app may vibrate your phone when it is level. It needs no special permission. It does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files, and it has no account or sign-up.

Settings stored on your phone

The app saves only your settings on your device: calibration values, units, sound and vibration choices, ruler calibration and "keep screen on". They may be included in your Android device backup if you have backups turned on. You can delete them at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and process data such as your device's Advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.

  • Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).
  • Learn how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Security

All network connections made by the app use encryption (HTTPS).

Your rights

Because the app does not collect personal data on our servers, we hold no personal data about you. For data processed by Google for ads, use the controls above or contact Google. For any question, email ouayatstudio@gmail.com.
